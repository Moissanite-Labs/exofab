# Agent guidance

Read [README](README.md), [orientation](docs/orientation.md), and the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) before contributing. Check current issues and pull requests for overlapping work.

- Treat the project direction and roadmap as early proposals. Do not present unimplemented capabilities or unresolved choices as decisions.
- Keep work focused and reviewable. Cite evidence, state assumptions, and report what was actually verified.
- Protect credentials, private context, and personal data. Do not place them in repository content or public discussions.
- Do not introduce a technology stack, service, dependency, license, or deployment as an established choice without a project decision. External research is reference material, not authorization to adopt it.
- Use fresh current sources and content-only review. Do not import another repository’s pre-cleanup ancestry or private historical material.

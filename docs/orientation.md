# Orientation

## What is Exofab?

Exofab is the proposed creation hub of Project Exo. The [Moissanite Labs overview](https://github.com/Moissanite-Labs/.github/blob/main/docs/index.html) describes a planned place for people and agents to collaborate on bringing projects, products, and companies into the world. The original repository README describes it as the creation hub of Project Exo. Neither establishes a product architecture or implemented capabilities.

## Starting here

1. Read the [roadmap and decision record](roadmap.md) and identify which questions remain open.
2. Look at the current repository and its issues and pull requests before starting work; avoid duplicating active work.
3. Propose a small, reviewable change with its evidence, assumptions, and open questions. See [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Questions to resolve

- Who is the first intended user, and what concrete creation journey should the first version support?
- What belongs in Exofab versus Exo and other related projects? What integration boundaries are needed?
- How should people and agents share context, make decisions, and hand work off? Which actions require human approval?
- What data, identity, access, privacy, and deployment requirements apply?
- What is the smallest useful first milestone, and how will its outcome be evaluated?
- Which technology stack, operating model, and license should the project use?

These are questions for project direction, not descriptions of existing features. External research can inform later comparison; it does not authorize adopting code, services, or an architecture.

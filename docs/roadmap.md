# Roadmap and decision record

This is a proposed sequence for discussion, not an approved delivery schedule or a claim that work is assigned.

1. **Frame the first use case.** Agree on the intended user, a concrete creation journey, desired outcome, and non-goals.
2. **Define boundaries.** Describe the roles of people and agents, relationships to Exo and other projects, data and authority boundaries, and decisions requiring human review.
3. **Design a first slice.** Compare approaches against the use case, document tradeoffs and open risks, and choose a small milestone with observable acceptance criteria.
4. **Build and evaluate.** After the direction and implementation choices are agreed, deliver and assess the first slice against those criteria.
